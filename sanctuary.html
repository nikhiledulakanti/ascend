Plan by season

Pick a season to see what the sanctuary is like and what to bring.

Spring: mild mornings and open shorelines

  • Mild air for early walks along the bay.
  • Quiet beaches before the summer season starts.
  • Pack layers for cool mornings and warm afternoons.

Summer: long light and warm water

  • The longest days, ideal for water activities.
  • Book early; this is the busiest season.
  • Bring sun protection and reef-safe sunscreen.

Autumn: clear skies and calm tides

  • Cooler air and some of the clearest views of the year.
  • Good conditions for long shoreline walks.
  • Pack a warm layer for evenings on the balcony.

Winter: quiet, reflective stays

  • The sanctuary at its most peaceful, with lower rates.
  • Cold, bright days are good for photography.
  • Confirm weather-dependent activities with the concierge.
